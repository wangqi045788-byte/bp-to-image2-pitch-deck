# Image2 prompt contract

Every slide prompt is a production specification, not a mood-board sentence.

## Required blocks

1. **Role and deliverable** — senior pitch-deck art director; one finished 16:9 slide.
2. **Objective and audience** — what the viewer must understand or believe.
3. **Exact visible copy** — title, labels, metrics, bullets, qualifiers, and source line. Mark text as exact and prohibit paraphrase or additions.
4. **Evidence lock** — evidence IDs and the factual elements that may not change.
5. **Reference assets** — asset IDs, intended placement, and preservation rules.
6. **Composition** — grid, zones, dominant object, reading path, balance, whitespace, and depth.
7. **Visual mechanism** — diagram, evidence frame, product hero, data view, process, comparison, or other page-specific treatment.
8. **Brand system** — palette, typography, shape language, imagery, material, and logo rules.
9. **Craft direction** — lighting, texture, line quality, chart treatment, icon semantics, and finish.
10. **Negative constraints** — forbidden styles, objects, invented content, and failure patterns.
11. **Acceptance criteria** — exact text, correct numbers, intact assets, hierarchy, legibility, aspect ratio, and no artifacts.

## Complexity rule

High complexity means a precise relationship among content, evidence, hierarchy, material, and composition. It does not mean more decoration. Specify enough geometry and semantics for Image2 to produce a controlled page while leaving room for visual craft.

## Exact-copy block

List every visible text string on its own line. Do not bury critical copy inside prose. Include punctuation, units, currency, capitalization, and line breaks where required.

## Prompt variation

Use the same global brand system across the deck, but vary page mechanics intentionally. Adjacent slides should not repeat an identical card grid. Preserve a coherent rhythm through shared margins, typography, palette, and evidence treatment.

## Regeneration instruction

For a failed slide, identify the smallest failing block and request a targeted revision. Do not repeatedly regenerate the entire visual direction for a local typo, alignment issue, or asset-preservation error.
